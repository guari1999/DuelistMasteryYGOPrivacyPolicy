Privacy Policy – Duelist Mastery YGO
Last updated: November 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Duelist Mastery YGO ("the App") handles user data. The App is developed, maintained, and controlled (Data Controller) by Luca Guarino, based in Italy.

For any privacy-related inquiries, you can contact us at: rulingmastery@gmail.com.

1. User Accounts and Authentication
The App does not require you to create a private account. However, it integrates with Google Play Games Services to provide social features such as:

Leaderboards: To rank your "Duelist EXP" against other players.

Achievements: To track your progress and milestones in the quiz.

When you use these features, your Google account identifier and game scores are transmitted to and stored on Google’s servers. You can manage this data or sign out at any time through the Google Play Games app or your Google Account settings.

2. Data Stored Locally on the Device and Permissions
Most of the data generated by your use of the App remains exclusively on your device and is never uploaded to our servers.

a. Duelist Profile and Quiz Progress: Information such as your chosen duelist name, current EXP, and Daily Streak progress is stored locally via Android DataStore.

b. Audio Recordings (Microphone Permission): The App requests microphone access solely to allow you to record duel audio when manually initiated by you. These files are stored exclusively in the App’s private storage on your device and are never transmitted or shared automatically.

c. User Images: Images selected from your gallery via the system picker for use as custom backgrounds remain only on the device.

d. PDF and Exported Files: PDFs of duel history or deck exports (.ydk files) are generated locally. Sharing these files is entirely user-initiated (e.g., via email or messaging apps).

3. Advertising and Consent
The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements to free users.

Data Collection: AdMob may collect device identifiers (Android Advertising ID), IP address, and usage data for ad personalization, fraud prevention, and analytics.

Consent Management: We use the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request your consent for data processing in compliance with GDPR. You can review or modify your privacy choices at any time within the App’s settings.

4. Offline Limit (Free Version)
To manage free tier usage, the free version tracks the number of quiz questions answered while offline. This counter is stored locally on your device and resets daily.

5. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services
We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties, except through the official SDKs integrated into the App:

Google Play Games: For score synchronization and rankings.

Google AdMob: For providing and measuring advertisements.

Google Play Billing: To securely process "PRO PASS" subscriptions (we never access or store your payment details).

For more information on how Google processes data, please review Google’s Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. Data Retention and User Rights (GDPR)
Local data is retained on your device until you clear the App's data or uninstall the App. Data processed by Google services is retained according to Google's standard retention policies.

Users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the UK have specific rights regarding their personal data (access, rectification, erasure, restriction). Since we do not store your data on our own servers, you can exercise your rights directly by:

Deleting Local Data: Clear the App's cache/storage in Android settings or uninstall the App to permanently remove all local data.

Managing Google Data: Use the Google "My Activity" or Google Play Games dashboard to manage or delete data stored on Google's servers.

Ad Preferences: Reset your Advertising ID in your device’s Google settings or update your consent inside the App.

7. Security
Data transfers to Google services (AdMob, Play Games, and Play Billing) are encrypted in transit via HTTPS/TLS. Local data is stored in Android's private application directory, inaccessible to other applications without root access.
